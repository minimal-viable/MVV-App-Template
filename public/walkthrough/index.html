How to give feedback

The best feedback is captured in the moment, against the screen in front of you.

1

Start a screen recording that also transcribes as you talk. Tools like Loom work well for this.

2

Click any card to open that screen in the platform.

3

Move through the cards in order. Where a card has an AI triggers button, open it and copy a phrase to see the agent work.

4

As you explore each page or flow, think aloud: say what you're thinking, your opinions, what looks good, what feels unnecessary. It's all captured on your screen-recording transcript. Then share the recording back with me on Basecamp chat, or by email to yasmin.petts@minimalviablelaunch.io.
